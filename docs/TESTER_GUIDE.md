# Tester Guide (`docs/TESTER_GUIDE.md`)

Thanks for trying Talking Drum VR on your Quest. This takes about 5 minutes. You don't need to
know anything about the game beforehand — just follow the steps below.

## 1. Open it

1. Put on your headset and open **Quest Browser**.
2. Go to: **`https://drum.da-costa.online/?debug=1`**
3. Sit down somewhere comfortable, with a bit of open space in front of you.
4. Tap **Enter VR**, then confirm the VR prompt if your browser asks.

The `?debug=1` in the address turns on a small on-screen readout you won't normally see — that's
intentional, it's how you'll send us your results at the end (step 7).

## 2. Six quick checks

Just play naturally — try a lesson, strike the drum, squeeze with your left hand — and keep these
six questions in mind as you go. You don't need to answer them precisely; just notice.

1. **Does a normal strike register?** When you tap the drum at a normal pace, does it count (you
   should hear a drum sound and see your strike count go up)?
2. **Any false or missed hits?** Does the drum ever sound when you didn't mean to strike it, or
   stay silent when you clearly did?
3. **Does squeezing your left hand reliably change the pitch — low, mid, and high?** Try a loose
   open hand, a relaxed middle squeeze, and a tight fist. Each should sound like a different pitch,
   and the pitch should track your squeeze fairly evenly.
4. **Any noticeable sound delay?** Does the drum sound happen right when your hand hits, or does it
   feel like there's a lag?
5. **Are the panels readable and reachable?** The word/lesson panel and the pitch ladder — can you
   read the text comfortably, and can you reach everything without leaning or stretching?
6. **Comfort.** Nothing about the posture, reach, or session length should feel physically
   uncomfortable. If something does, that's useful to know.

There's no need to finish every lesson or get everything right — we want your honest first
impression, including mistakes and confusion.

## 3. Exit and copy your report

1. When you're done (a few minutes is plenty), tap **Exit VR**.
2. You'll see a small debug panel in the corner of the browser window. Tap the **Copy report**
   button in it.
3. This copies a plain-text summary of your session (no personal data, nothing sent over the
   network — it's just copied to your device's clipboard).

## 4. Send it back

Paste the copied report, plus a few sentences of your own notes, back to whoever asked you to
test. Please try to cover:

- Your own answers to the six checks above, in your own words.
- Anything that surprised you, confused you, or felt off.
- Your headset model, if you know it, and roughly how tall you are / whether you were seated or
  standing (this helps us tune the drum's placement).

## The three things we most want to know

If you only tell us three things, make them these:

1. **Did strikes feel reliable?** (Check #1 and #2 above — too many misses or false hits is the
   single biggest thing we need to fix.)
2. **Did the left-hand pitch squeeze feel controllable?** (Check #3 — this is the part of the game
   we have the least real-headset data on.)
3. **Was anything physically uncomfortable, or out of reach?** (Check #5 and #6 — reach and comfort
   are hard to judge without a real headset, which is exactly why we're asking you.)

Thank you — this kind of real-device feedback is something we can't get any other way.
